# Brainstorming Technique Catalog

62 techniques across 10 categories. Load this file when facilitating brainstorming and selecting techniques.

---

## Collaborative Techniques (7)

| # | Name | Description | Energy | Duration |
|---|------|-------------|--------|----------|
| 07 | Brainwriting | Silent written ideation where participants write ideas and pass papers to build on others' thoughts. | Low | 25 min |
| 08 | Round Robin | Each person contributes one idea in turn, building momentum and ensuring equal participation. | Low | 20 min |
| 51 | Silent Brainstorming | Extended silent writing period before any discussion. Prevents groupthink. | Low | 25 min |
| 52 | Gallery Walk | Post ideas on walls, participants walk around adding comments and building on others. | Medium | 30 min |
| 53 | Speed Dating Ideas | Pair up, share ideas for 2 minutes, switch partners. Rapid cross-pollination. | High | 20 min |
| 54 | Fishbowl | Inner circle discusses while outer circle observes, then switch. Multiple perspective layers. | Medium | 30 min |
| 55 | Dotmocracy | Everyone gets dot stickers to vote on favorite ideas. Visual democratic prioritization. | Low | 15 min |

## Deep Techniques (9)

| # | Name | Description | Energy | Duration |
|---|------|-------------|--------|----------|

| 16 | Assumption Busting | List all assumptions about the problem, then challenge each one to find hidden opportunities. | Low | 25 min |

| 59 | Root Cause Tree | Branch out causes from the problem, then causes of causes, building a tree of underlying issues. | Low | 30 min |

## Quick Selection Guide

| Goal | Recommended Techniques |
|------|----------------------|
| Root cause analysis | 5 Whys, First Principles, Root Cause Tree |
| Rapid idea generation | Crazy Eights, Round Robin, Brainwriting |
| Systematic exploration | SCAMPER, Morphological Analysis, Attribute Listing |
| Perspective shifting | Role Storming, Six Thinking Hats, Alien Perspective |
| Breaking constraints | Constraint Elimination, 10x Thinking, Assumption Busting |
| Team alignment | Dotmocracy, Gallery Walk, Fishbowl |
| Default quick start | 5 Whys + Mind Mapping |
